What is an Ideathon?

An Ideathon is a short, intensive, brainstorming event where people come together to address tough challenges. Unlike a traditional hackathon, the focus here is strictly on problem-solving, ideation, and strategy, rather than immediate coding or engineering.

Innovation Tracks

Teams can pitch solutions focusing on the following core domains:

AI for Social Good
Using machine learning and artificial intelligence to address community challenges.
Smart Public Services
Building solutions for seamless urban transportation, safety, and infrastructure.
Sustainability & Climate
Developing eco-friendly tech to track and lower carbon emissions.
